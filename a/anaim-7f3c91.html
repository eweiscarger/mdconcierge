One question for you

Will you authorise the pharmacist to fill diclofenac 1% as diclofenac 1.5% as a standing instruction, so we are not calling your office every time?

Before you answer: the 1.5% we carry is a solution, not a gel, so this changes the form as well as the strength. Entirely your call, and nothing changes until you say so.

Keep this on your phone

It lives at a web address rather than in a file, so it is always the current version. Two taps and it sits on your home screen like an app.

iPhone. Tap the share button at the bottom of Safari, the square with the arrow coming out of it. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android. Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome, then Add to Home screen, then Install or Add.

Either one. If you would rather not, just bookmark it, or leave the text where it is and open it from there.
