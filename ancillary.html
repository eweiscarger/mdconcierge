How it works

Tell us what you are considering.

Most practices know roughly what they want to add and have no time to evaluate vendors, negotiate terms or manage the rollout. That is the part we take.

1

Tell us what you are considering

A service you have been asked about, a gap you keep seeing, or something a colleague mentioned.

2

We source and vet

We find the vendors who actually serve your specialty and your state, and we tell you which ones do not fit.

3

We negotiate the terms

You are not the one on the phone working out pricing, minimums or service levels.

4

Set up under your name

The program runs as part of your practice, not as a vendor operating next to it.

5

We stay on it

One point of contact afterward instead of a different rep for every service line.

How we work

Coordination, not obligation.

We represent the vendors behind these services as well as the practices that use them. That is exactly why the next two lines matter.

You are never required to use a vendor we bring. If you already have someone good, keep them. We will say so.
We will tell you when something is not right for you. A service that does not fit your patient population is not worth setting up.
One point of contact. Instead of six vendor reps calling your office independently.
Nothing lands on your staff. Setup, paperwork and follow-up are handled outside your front desk.
