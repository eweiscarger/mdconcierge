Workers' Compensation Pharmacy Platform

Share in the revenue from the work-comp scripts you already write, without owning or running a pharmacy.

A turnkey program for Pennsylvania practices that treat injured workers. You keep prescribing exactly as you do today. We handle everything else.

How it works

1
You e-prescribe in your EHR, exactly as you do today.
2
An independent, licensed mail-order pharmacy ships the medication to your patient's home, next day, at no cost to them, with no denials while the claim is open.
3
Through the Platform, your practice shares in the revenue from those work-comp prescriptions. You participate without owning or running a pharmacy.
4
We handle the pharmacy operations, billing, collections, compliance, customer service, and patient support. Your staff touches none of it.

What you get

  • Better medication adherence for your injured workers, delivered to their door at no cost.
  • A share of the revenue from the prescriptions you are already writing, instead of referring them elsewhere.
  • No workflow change and no administrative burden for you or your staff.
  • Ten years of experience behind the operations, collections, compliance, and service.

The legal foundation

On June 16, 2026, in 700 Pharmacy v. Bureau of Workers' Compensation Fee Review, the Pennsylvania Supreme Court confirmed that physicians may have a financial interest in a workers' compensation pharmacy, and that insurers cannot deny payment for covered prescriptions on that basis.
