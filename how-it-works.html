How the program works

If you treat injured workers, you're already generating these claims. Here's how to keep the economics.

Your patients get their medication delivered instead of chasing a counter that may not stock it. The denials and prior authorizations stop landing on your desk. And the benefit stays with you and the patient rather than a pharmacy benefits manager not involved in the treatment at all.

It is entirely mail order. The pharmacy bills the carrier on the state fee schedule and handles collections. You stock nothing, add no staff, and never touch a patient payment. Physicians participate individually or through their practice, whichever suits their situation.
