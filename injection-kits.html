Service line 03

Single-use injection kits.

Corticosteroid, local anesthetic and NSAID kits for in-office injections. Each kit holds the drug and the supplies for one injection, so nothing is drawn from a shared vial and nothing sits on your shelf between patients.

What we carry

Four kinds of kit.

Single-use kits across the injections a pain, orthopaedic or physiatry practice performs most. Single and double-dose versions where the injection calls for it.

Corticosteroid with anesthetic

Methylprednisolone, triamcinolone or betamethasone paired with lidocaine in a single kit.

  • Methylprednisolone 40mg · Triamcinolone 40mg · Betamethasone 6mg/mL

Corticosteroid only

Dexamethasone kits, single and double dose.

  • Dexamethasone 10mg/mL · double-dose dexamethasone

Local anesthetic

Standalone anesthetic kits for infiltration and diagnostic blocks.

  • Lidocaine · Bupivacaine 0.5%

NSAID

Ketorolac kits, single and double dose, for non-narcotic pain control.

  • Ketorolac 30mg/mL · double-dose ketorolac

Why single use

One kit, one patient.

The case for single-use kits is mostly about what they remove from the room: preparation, shared vials, and ambiguity about what a given patient received.

One kit, one injection. The drug and the supplies for a single injection arrive together in one sealed kit.
Nothing drawn from a shared vial. Each patient gets a sealed single-use kit rather than a draw from a multi-dose vial.
Lot traceable to the patient. One kit maps to one patient, so the lot number in the chart is unambiguous.
Nothing to prepare. Kits arrive ready to use, so no time goes into drawing up or labelling between patients.
Nothing expiring on the shelf. You stock the kits you use, in the quantities you use them.
Ordering handled. Usage is tracked and reorders are placed before you run short.
