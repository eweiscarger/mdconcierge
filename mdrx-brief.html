Who We Are

A workers' compensation pharmacy program that ensures injured workers receive the medication their physician prescribed.

MDRx is a workers' compensation billing and revenue-cycle firm. Our pharmacy claims-purchasing program resolves a persistent gap in work-comp care: patients who cannot obtain their prescribed medication. A network of independent pharmacies fills and ships every prescription, while MDRx assumes billing and collections in full.
