Service line 01

Referral management and medical coordination.

The core of what we do. Attorneys send a client once. Providers receive a patient whose claim is already verified. We stay on the case in between, so neither side has to chase the other.

Email us Join the network
How a referral moves

Send it once. We take it from there.

One email starts it. Every step after that happens on our side, and both the firm and the practice are told where things stand without having to ask.

1

Email the referral

Any format. A forwarded intake, a name and a phone number, or a full packet. There is no portal and no form to learn.

2

Claim verified

Carrier, adjuster, claim number and NCP confirmed before anything is placed, so a referral does not come back.

3

Matched and placed

Matched to a practice by specialty, geography and claim status, with a provider who has already accepted the claim.

4

Introductions made

Both sides receive full contact information and the appointment. Nobody is left guessing who to call.

5

Ongoing coordination

Records requests, carrier relays, ancillary needs and status updates continue through the same address for the life of the case.

How it works

Simple on your end. Coordinated on ours.

Providers are our clients, and attorneys refer into the same desk. We handle the placements, follow-ups, status pings and paperwork that nobody on either side wants to chase.

For Attorneys

Send a client once. MDconcierge matches, places and coordinates, with one-click action buttons on every email so you can request anything in seconds.

  1. 1
    Email the referralSend client details to referrals@mdconcierge.net. We ask eight quick follow-up questions and you reply once.
  2. 2
    We match and placeYour client is matched to an in-network provider by specialty, geography and claim status.
  3. 3
    Introduction sentOnce the provider accepts, both parties are introduced with full contact on each side.
  4. 4
    Ongoing coordinationStatus pings, ancillary referrals and carrier relays, all through email.
What your firm gets
  • Your client is seen quickly, by a provider who has already accepted the claim
  • Nobody at your firm chases a practice for an appointment or a status
  • Claim details, carrier and NCP confirmed before placement, so a referral does not come back
  • Ancillary needs, records requests and carrier relays handled from the same address
  • Lien-based coordination where the case calls for it
  • No cost to your firm, ever

For Providers

Receive matched work comp and personal injury patients with full claim information before you accept. NCP on file, carrier confirmed, liability verified. No surprises.

  1. 1
    Case notificationA structured notification with claim status, NCP, carrier, body parts and prior treatment. Contact stays locked until you accept.
  2. 2
    Accept, contact revealedOne reply. Patient phone, date of birth and attorney contact revealed immediately, with an EHR import packet included.
  3. 3
    Introduction sentYou and the attorney are introduced directly, and both sides have full contact from that point forward.
  4. 4
    Status pingsThirty-day check-ins. Reply with one letter and the attorney gets a clean summary. Your chart stays in your office.
What your practice gets
  • Claim, carrier and NCP confirmed before you are asked to accept
  • You decide which cases fit your practice, every time
  • Scheduling and follow-up handled so your front desk is not absorbing it
  • Records and carrier correspondence routed through one address
  • Ancillary services coordinated alongside the referral when you want them
