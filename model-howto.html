How to use your dispensing analysis

Under a minute. Everything in it is yours to change.

  1. Pick your state. Every state sets its own workers' compensation pharmacy fee schedule, and they are not close to each other. Pennsylvania pays AWP plus 10%. New York pays AWP minus 12% on brand and minus 20% on generic. Choosing your state sets the multiplier for you, and you can type your own number over it any time.
  2. Set your monthly patient volume, then add the medications you would actually dispense and how many of each an average patient receives per month.
  3. Read the estimate. It updates as you type. The defaults are deliberately conservative: 80% collections on orals, 70% on topicals, and your practice keeping 65% of net. Change any of them to match how your practice really runs.

The full formulary, including what the practice pays for each product, sits at the bottom of the tool. These are the most common medications only. If you dispense something that is not on the list, say so and it gets added.
