Private, prepared for one practice, and not for distribution. Reimbursement is the Delaware and Pennsylvania workers’ compensation pharmacy fee schedules as published in the Optum Workers’ Compensation Pharmacy Resource Guide, March 2026: Delaware pays average wholesale price less 38 percent plus $4.10, Pennsylvania pays average wholesale price plus 10 percent. Medication prices and acquisition costs are the PDRx formulary. Claim purchase fees are the medication, a $5.00 dispense fee and $8.50 shipping, deducted from collections, which average about sixty days. The practice is accountable for the cost of goods and the fees on every fill. Net income is divided evenly.

This is arithmetic on published schedules and a good-faith estimate, not a promise of an amount. The carrier decides what it pays and when. The program is 100 percent mail order: you prescribe, and an independent licensed mail-order pharmacy fills and ships to the patient. Nothing is dispensed in your office. MDconcierge is not a pharmacy and does not practice medicine. Nothing in this document is a recommendation about how to treat any patient. What to prescribe, for whom, and in what quantity is yours alone. Clinical questions go to the pharmacist.
