PDRx Mail Order Pharmacy

What the PDRx program is worth to your practice

Workers' compensation medication, mailed to the patient, billed on your state's pharmacy fee schedule.

1. Your state

Every state sets its own workers' comp pharmacy reimbursement, as a multiple of AWP. Pennsylvania pays AWP + 10%. Pick yours.

2. What you would prescribe in a month

Tick anything in the formulary below, as many as you like, and it lands here. Then set how many scripts a month. Nothing here is a commitment. Injection kits are shown for reference and cannot be added.

3. The agreement

These are the PDRx terms. They are shown so the arithmetic is yours to check, not something you have to take on trust.

Claims purchase fee, deducted from collections: actual wholesale cost of the medication, plus the $5.00 pharmacy dispense fee, plus actual shipping, which runs $7.00 to $10.00 and is modelled here at $8.50.
Management fee: 50% of net income, where net income is gross collections less claims purchase fees. The practice keeps the other 50%.
Timing: collections average about 60 days.
