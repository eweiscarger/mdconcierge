How it works

Mail order pharmacy with workers’ comp / PIP

01

PATIENT VISITS THE PHYSICIAN’S OFFICE.

02

PHYSICIAN PLACES DRUG ORDER with PDRX at the patient’s direction.

03

PDRX SHIPS DRUGS directly to the patient.

04

PHYSICIAN PAYS PDRX fair market value (FMV) for assignment of drug receivable.

05

PHYSICIAN ASSIGNS TO ELITE CARE the right to bill and collect for services.

5.1  Elite Care provides payment, billing, and collection services.

5.2  Elite Care bills the WC/PIP payor for the claim.

06

WC/PIP PAYOR PAYS ELITE CARE.

6.1  Elite Care deposits payment into the physician’s account (less Elite Care’s fee).

Operating detail

How the program runs

Medication purchasing power

Direct purchasing relationships. Manufacturers and customers always pay wholesale with NO mark-up.

Prescribing options

EMR script transfer (preferred), web-based portal, and fax.

Shipping

The first script written for a patient is always sent overnight. Refills are filled on the 28th day to prevent delays or running out of medication. For special cases, such as surgical patients, include a note in the EMR script transfer, and the pharmacy can expedite shipping.

Communication

Email is the preferred method of communication; however, we do have staff at the pharmacy from 8:00 AM–5:00 PM EST Monday–Friday.

Workers’ compensation network contracts

Helios, Coventry, Express Scripts, Optum, My Matrix, Mitchell, Corvel, and many more.

Questions

Frequently asked

How long does it take to receive medication?

Typically, it takes between 24–48 hours to receive medication. If Meds are ordered on a Friday, they will generally be received the following Monday.

Do you ship controlled C2 pain meds?

No, we believe that there is no place in pharmacy to ship C2s and that these types of meds should be dealt with by walk-in pharmacies.

What about surgical patients?

If a surgical patient is indicated on the prescription, we can increase the shipping speed to next day or overnight. There will be an increase in cost for express delivery.

When are reports delivered?

Accounting is based on a true calendar month that starts on the first day of each month and runs through the last day. Reports will be sent to you by the 15th of each month.

What are the payment options?

Both check and ACH options are available. A client who wishes to be set up on ACH simply needs to provide a check with the routing number and account number.

What happens if the meds are stolen or the patient does not receive the meds?

FedEx has images of delivery and tracking available. We will do everything on our end to ensure the patient receives the meds prescribed. In the event of theft or if a package is not delivered, we will express ship a replacement.
