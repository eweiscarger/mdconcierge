The formulary

Select and customize your own formulary so you can see what it looks like for you or for the practice. It opens under your name, so it is yours rather than a generic list.

  1. Sign in with your name and email. That is all it asks for, and it is what puts your name on the analysis.
  2. Set the assumptions at the top. Patients per month, and your state, which sets the multiplier from that state's workers' compensation pharmacy fee schedule. Dispensing fee, shipping and the collection percentages are all editable if you want to be more or less conservative than the defaults.
  3. Build your mix. Add the medications you actually prescribe and the estimate updates as you go.
  4. The full formulary is at the bottom, searchable by product, category or route, with the injection kits below it.
