Sending a work comp prescription to Excel

For whoever handles the scripts. Two minutes to set up, then it is one click a visit. Nothing about how you prescribe changes.

The rule

When the visit is work comp, the prescription goes to Excel Pharmacy rather than the patient’s retail pharmacy. That is the whole change.

Save it as a favorite once it comes up. Next time it is one click instead of six, and that is what decides whether this is still happening in three months.

Two ways to send it

  1. E-prescribe. Route the script to Excel Pharmacy from your existing e-prescribing software, exactly as you would to any other pharmacy.
  2. Or fax to 877 274 1122, together with a completed demographic sheet.

What the demographic sheet must include

This is what holds prescriptions up. Six items:

  • Diagnosis code
  • Claim number
  • Allergies, or NKA
  • Date of injury
  • Work comp insurance carrier
  • Adjuster and phone

What happens next

Excel’s advocate team calls the patient within 24 hours to confirm insurance, the prescription and the delivery address. The call comes from area code 484 or 267, which is worth telling the patient so they answer it.

From there it ships to their home. No copay and nothing paid out of pocket for covered work comp medications, no trip to a counter, and no prior authorization landing on your staff. Patients get their medication while the claim is open.

What this office never does

Nothing is stocked here, nothing is handled here, and nothing is billed from here. MDRx does the billing and the collections.
