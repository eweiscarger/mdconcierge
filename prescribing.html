MDRx workers’ compensation pharmacy

Sending a work comp prescription to Excel

For whoever handles the scripts. Two minutes to set up, then it is one click a visit. Nothing about how you prescribe changes.

The rule

When the visit is work comp, the prescription goes to Excel Pharmacy Services rather than the patient’s retail pharmacy. That is the whole change.

Your email, to open this

This is specific to your practice, so it is not public. Put in your email and it opens.

Set it up once

  1. Search your pharmacy directory for Excel Pharmacy Services. It is in the same directory as every other pharmacy you send to.
  2. Widen the search past your local area. Most systems only list nearby pharmacies until you turn that off. A mail order pharmacy will not appear until you do, and this is the step practices miss.
  3. Check it accepts electronic prescriptions, which your system will mark in the results.
  4. Save it as a favorite or preferred pharmacy. Next time it is one click instead of six, and that is what decides whether this is still happening in three months.

Every time after that

  1. Work comp visit, select Excel Pharmacy Services.
  2. Prescribe exactly as you always do.
  3. Glance at the pharmacy on the order before you sign it.

What the patient gets

It ships to their home overnight with nothing to pay. No trip to a counter that will not take a comp claim, and no prior authorization landing on your staff. If the prescription comes in, it gets dispensed.

What this office never does

Nothing is stocked here, nothing is handled here, and nothing is billed from here. MDRx does the billing and the collections.
